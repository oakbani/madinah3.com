1 · The ruling at a glance

Cover from just beneath the navel to the knee

ʿAwrah means the parts of the body that must be covered in front of other people and in prayer.

The ruling

A man must cover from just beneath the navel to the knee. Shorts that leave part of the thigh bare do not cover enough.

The navel itself need not be covered, but the shorts should start just beneath it. The Hanafi school goes one step further: the knee must be covered too, so the shorts should go below the kneecap.
Exposing the thighs is forbidden according to all four schools.

Also: the cloth must be thick enough that the colour of the skin does not show through.

2 · The Hanafi view

For the Hanafis, the knee must be covered too

The Hanafi school is one of the four main schools of Islamic law. ʿAwrah means the parts of the body that must be covered in front of other people and in prayer.

Hanafi verdict

A man must cover from below the navel to below the knee. The knee is included, so shorts should go below the kneecap.

وَالْعَوْرَةُ مِنَ الرَّجُلِ: مَا تَحْتَ السُّرَّةِ إِلَى الرُّكْبَةِ وَالرُّكْبَةُ مِنَ الْعَوْرَةِ
"A man's ʿawrah is what is below the navel up to the knee, and the knee is part of the ʿawrah."
al-Qudūrī, Mukhtaṣar al-Qudūrī p. 26 link

Why is the knee included?

1. The wording of the hadith

Two narrations are mentioned: "from his navel to his knee", and "below his navel until he passes his knee". The Hanafis read the word "to" in the first one as "with", so the knee is counted. (al-Hidāya)

وَكَلِمَةُ إِلَى تَحْمِلُهَا عَلَى كَلِمَةِ مَعَ عَمَلًا بِكَلِمَةِ حَتَّى
"The word 'to' is taken to mean 'with', acting on the word 'until'."
al-Marghīnānī, al-Hidāya 1/45 link

2. The safe side

A limit may or may not include its end point. Since this is a matter of caution, the Hanafis count the knee in.

الْغَايَةَ قَدْ تَدْخُلُ وَقَدْ تَخْرُجُ وَالْمَوْضِعُ مَوْضِعُ الِاحْتِيَاطِ فَحَكَمْنَا بِدُخُولِهَا احْتِيَاطًا
"A limit may be included or left out, and this is a place of caution, so we ruled that it is included, out of caution."
Ibn al-Humām, Fatḥ al-Qadīr 1/258 link

3. The knee is where two bones meet

The knee joins the thigh bone and the shin bone, and the two cannot be told apart. When something permitted and something forbidden meet, the forbidden side wins.

وَلِأَنَّ الرُّكْبَةَ مُلْتَقَى عَظْمَيِ السَّاقِ وَالْفَخِذِ وَالتَّمْيِيزُ بَيْنَهُمَا مُتَعَذِّرٌ فَاجْتَمَعَ الْمُحَرِّمُ وَالْمُبِيحُ فَغَلَبَ الْمُحَرِّمُ احْتِيَاطًا
"Because the knee is where the bones of the shin and the thigh meet, and telling them apart is not possible, what is forbidden and what is permitted came together, and the forbidden prevailed, out of caution."
Ibn Nujaym, al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq 1/284 link

4. The knees go with the thighs

Ibn ʿĀbidīn (Radd al-Muḥtār, also known as Fatāwā Shāmī) repeats the same limit, says it applies in prayer and outside it, and lists the two thighs together with the two knees among the parts a man must cover.

وَهِيَ لِلرَّجُلِ مَا تَحْتَ سُرَّتِهِ إِلَى مَا تَحْتَ رُكْبَتِهِ
"For a man it is what is below his navel to below his knee."
Radd al-Muḥtār 1/404 link
السَّادِسُ وَالسَّابِعُ الْفَخِذَانِ مَعَ الرُّكْبَتَيْنِ
"The sixth and the seventh: the two thighs with the two knees."
Radd al-Muḥtār 1/409 link

How serious is each part?

Hanafi scholars treat the knee as the lightest case. If someone sees a man with his knee uncovered, he should advise him gently; for the thigh, firmly; for the private parts, the man should be ordered to cover and disciplined if he insists.

حُكْمُ الْعَوْرَةِ فِي الرُّكْبَةِ أَخَفُّ مِنْهُ فِي الْفَخِذِ حَتَّى لَوْ رَأَى رَجُلٌ غَيْرَهُ مَكْشُوفَ الرُّكْبَةِ يُنْكِرُ عَلَيْهِ بِرِفْقٍ
"The ruling of ʿawrah in the knee is lighter than in the thigh, so much so that if a man sees another with his knee uncovered, he rebukes him gently."
al-Baḥr al-Rāʾiq 1/284 link
3 · If someone does not cover

What follows from uncovering the ʿawrah

It is forbidden

Uncovering in front of anyone other than one's wife is haram.

It is a major sin

Counted as a major sin by Shafiʿi scholars.

Prayer is not valid

Praying with part of the ʿawrah uncovered makes the prayer invalid.

His word as a witness may be refused

Malik and al-Shafiʿī reject the testimony of a man seen uncovered.

Correction by the authorities

The authorities may discipline him.

The unseen witnesses

The angels are always with us.

4 · Evidences in Shariah

What the Qurʾan and the Sunnah say about covering

Qurʾan The verses

يَـٰبَنِىٓ ءَادَمَ قَدْ أَنزَلْنَا عَلَيْكُمْ لِبَاسًا يُوَٰرِى سَوْءَٰتِكُمْ وَرِيشًا ۖ وَلِبَاسُ ٱلتَّقْوَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ خَيْرٌ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ مِنْ ءَايَـٰتِ ٱللَّهِ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَذَّكَّرُونَ
"O children of Adam, We have bestowed upon you clothing to conceal your private parts and as adornment. But the clothing of righteousness - that is best. That is from the signs of Allāh that perhaps they will remember."
al-Aʿrāf 7:26 · Translation: Saheeh International (Quran.com)
يَـٰبَنِىٓ ءَادَمَ لَا يَفْتِنَنَّكُمُ ٱلشَّيْطَـٰنُ كَمَآ أَخْرَجَ أَبَوَيْكُم مِّنَ ٱلْجَنَّةِ يَنزِعُ عَنْهُمَا لِبَاسَهُمَا لِيُرِيَهُمَا سَوْءَٰتِهِمَآ ۗ إِنَّهُۥ يَرَىٰكُمْ هُوَ وَقَبِيلُهُۥ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا تَرَوْنَهُمْ ۗ إِنَّا جَعَلْنَا ٱلشَّيَـٰطِينَ أَوْلِيَآءَ لِلَّذِينَ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ
"O children of Adam, let not Satan tempt you as he removed your parents from Paradise, stripping them of their clothing to show them their private parts. Indeed, he sees you, he and his tribe, from where you do not see them. Indeed, We have made the devils allies to those who do not believe."
al-Aʿrāf 7:27 · Translation: Saheeh International (Quran.com)
يَـٰبَنِىٓ ءَادَمَ خُذُوا۟ زِينَتَكُمْ عِندَ كُلِّ مَسْجِدٍ وَكُلُوا۟ وَٱشْرَبُوا۟ وَلَا تُسْرِفُوٓا۟ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُسْرِفِينَ
"O children of Adam, take your adornment [i.e., wear your clothing] at every masjid, and eat and drink, but be not excessive. Indeed, He likes not those who commit excess."
al-Aʿrāf 7:31 · Translation: Saheeh International (Quran.com)
قُل لِّلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَغُضُّوا۟ مِنْ أَبْصَـٰرِهِمْ وَيَحْفَظُوا۟ فُرُوجَهُمْ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَزْكَىٰ لَهُمْ ۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ خَبِيرٌۢ بِمَا يَصْنَعُونَ
"Tell the believing men to reduce [some] of their vision and guard their private parts. That is purer for them. Indeed, Allāh is [fully] Aware of what they do."
al-Nūr 24:30 · Translation: Saheeh International (Quran.com)

"A man must not look at another man's ʿawrah."
Narrated by Abu Saʿīd al-Khudrī · Muslim · Mawsūʿat al-Fiqh 14/199
يَا مَعْمَرُ غَطِّ فَخِذَيْكَ فَإِنَّ الْفَخِذَيْنِ عَوْرَةٌ
"O Maʿmar, cover your thighs, for the thighs are ʿawrah."

Hadith Hadith that stress covering
