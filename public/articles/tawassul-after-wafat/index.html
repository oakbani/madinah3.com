يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ

Can We Call upon the Prophet ﷺ
After His Passing?

The view of the Ahl al-Sunnah scholars who allow it, with the evidence from the Qurʾān, the hadith and the scholars of every generation.

Who this page is about: people who believe that Allah alone is the real doer, and that the Prophet ﷺ acts only by Allah's permission. Anyone who believes the Prophet ﷺ acts independently of Allah commits shirk.
1
The Qurʾān

Come to the Messenger, ask forgiveness, and he asks for you

وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَا مِن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا لِيُطَاعَ بِإِذْنِ ٱللَّهِ ۚ وَلَوْ أَنَّهُمْ إِذ ظَّلَمُوٓا۟ أَنفُسَهُمْ جَآءُوكَ فَٱسْتَغْفَرُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ وَٱسْتَغْفَرَ لَهُمُ ٱلرَّسُولُ لَوَجَدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ تَوَّابًا رَّحِيمًا
“And We did not send any messenger except to be obeyed by permission of Allāh. And if, when they wronged themselves, they had come to you, [O Muḥammad], and asked forgiveness of Allāh and the Messenger had asked forgiveness for them, they would have found Allāh Accepting of Repentance and Merciful.”
Sūrat al-Nisāʾ 4:64 · Translation: Saheeh International (Quran.com)

What the commentators wrote on this verse

Ibn KathīrTafsīr 4/140
يرشد تعالى العصاة والمذنبين إذا وقع منهم الخطأ والعصيان أن يأتوا إلى الرسول ﷺ فيستغفروا اللَّه عنده ويسألوه أن يستغفر لهم
Allah guides sinners, when they fall into error, to come to the Messenger ﷺ, ask Allah's forgiveness in his presence and ask him to seek forgiveness for them. He then relates the well-known story of the man at the Prophet's grave. Shamela
al-Qurṭubī · al-Nasafī · Abū ḤayyānTafsīr 5/265 · Madārik 1/370 · al-Baḥr 3/694
قدم علينا أعرابي بعد ما دفنا رسول اللَّه ﷺ بثلاثة أيام… فنودي من القبر إنه قد غفر لك
All three relate that a Bedouin came after the Prophet's burial (al-Qurṭubī and Abū Ḥayyān, from ʿAlī: three days after), threw himself on the grave, recited this verse and asked for forgiveness, and a voice came from the grave: “You have been forgiven.” Qurṭubī · Nasafī · Abū Ḥayyān
al-Subkīquoted in al-Ṣārim al-Munkī, p. 314–315
فهمَ العلماءُ من الآيةِ العمومَ في الحالتين، واستحبوا لمن أتى قبرَ النبي ﷺ أن يتلوَ هذه الآيةَ ويستغفرَ اللَّه
“The scholars understood the verse to cover both cases [his life and after his death], and recommended that whoever comes to the Prophet's grave recite this verse and ask Allah's forgiveness.” Shamela
2
The hadith, one by one

The Prophet ﷺ taught a call that names him

Tirmidhī · Nasāʾī · Ibn Mājah The blind man

A blind man asked the Prophet ﷺ to pray for his sight. The Prophet ﷺ told him to make wuḍūʾ well, pray two rakʿahs and say:

اللَّهُمَّ إنِّي أسْألُكَ وأتَوَجَّهُ إلَيْكَ بِنَبِيِّكَ مُحَمَّدٍ نَبِيِّ الرَّحْمَةِ، يَا مُحَمَّدُ إنِّي تَوَجَّهْتُ بِكَ إِلَى رَبِّي فِي حاجَتِي هَذِهِ لِتُقْضَى لِي، اللَّهُمَّ فَشَفِّعْهُ فِيَّ
“O Allah, I ask You and turn to You by Your Prophet Muḥammad, the Prophet of mercy. O Muḥammad, I turn by you to my Lord in this need of mine, so that it be met. O Allah, accept his intercession for me.” The man got up able to see. ʿUthmān b. Ḥunayf said: “By Allah, we had not parted and the talk had not grown long before the man came back in, as if he had never been harmed.” (al-Bayhaqī, Dalāʾil 6/167)
Graded: al-Tirmidhī “ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ gharīb” · al-Ḥākim “ṣaḥīḥ on the condition of al-Bukhārī and Muslim” · al-Ṭabarānī “the hadith is ṣaḥīḥ” · al-Shawkānī: it proves tawassul by the Prophet ﷺ “with the belief that the Doer is Allah” (Tuḥfat al-Dhākirīn p. 212). Sources: Musnad Aḥmad 28/480 · al-Ḥākim, Mustadrak 2/761 · Jāmiʿ al-Masānīd 6/45
About weak reports: “weak” is not “made up”. Only fabricated reports are left out. A weak report can support that something is permitted, not that it is obligatory or forbidden. Marked weak.

MuslimAbū Dāwūd His Ummah's deeds are shown to him; his salām is answered

عُرِضَتْ عَلَيَّ أَعْمَالُ أُمَّتِي حَسَنُهَا وَسَيِّئُهَا
ما مِن أحدٍ يُسلِّمُ عليَّ إلا ردَّ اللَّهُ عليَّ روحي حتى أردَّ عليه السلام
“My Ummah’s deeds, good and bad, were shown to me.” (Abū Dharr, Muslim no. 553) · “No one greets me but Allah returns my soul to me so I return his greeting.” (Abū Hurayrah, Abū Dāwūd, in al-Mughnī 5/465)

Ibn Abī Shaybah · al-Bayhaqī The man at the grave in ʿUmar's time

أصابَ الناسَ قحطٌ في زمن عمر بن الخطاب فجاء رجلٌ إلى قبر النبي ﷺ فقال: يا رسولَ الله استسقِ اللهَ لأمتك فإنهم قد هَلَكوا. فأتاه رسول الله ﷺ في المنام فقال: ائت عمر فأقرئه مني السلامَ وأخبرهم أنهم مُسْقَوْن
“Famine struck the people in the time of ʿUmar. A man came to the grave of the Prophet ﷺ and said: ‘O Messenger of Allah, ask Allah for rain for your Ummah, for they are perishing.’ Then the Messenger of Allah ﷺ came to him in a dream and said: ‘Go to ʿUmar, give him my greetings, and tell them that they will be given rain.’”
Via Mālik al-Dār, ʿUmar’s freed servant · Ibn Abī Shaybah 18/31 · al-Bayhaqī, Dalāʾil al-Nubuwwah 7/47
Ibn Ḥajar: “with a ṣaḥīḥ chain”; he names the man as the Companion Bilāl b. al-Ḥārith al-Muzanī (Fatḥ al-Bārī 2/495, 2/496) · al-Bayhaqī: “isnād ṣaḥīḥ” (al-Bidāyah 7/205)

al-Ṭabarānī ʿUthmān b. Ḥunayf teaches the du'a after the Prophet's death grading disputed

ثُمَّ قُلْ: اللهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ وَأَتَوَجَّهُ إِلَيْكَ بِنَبِيِّنَا مُحَمَّدٍ نَبِيِّ الرَّحْمَةِ، يَا مُحَمَّدُ إِنِّي أَتَوَجَّهُ بِكَ إِلَى رَبِّي فَتَقْضِي لِي حَاجَتِي
In ʿUthmān ibn ʿAffān's time a man's need was not being met. The Companion ʿUthmān b. Ḥunayf told him to pray two rakʿahs and say the du'a above. The man did, and his need was met. al-Muʿjam al-Kabīr 9/30
Ibn Taymiyya: al-Bayhaqī’s story “may be used as evidence by one who makes tawassul after his death, if it is ṣaḥīḥ” (Majmūʿ al-Fatāwā 1/268); al-Albānī calls the story weak (Mawsūʿat al-ʿAqīdah 3/680).

al-Bukhārī, al-Adab al-Mufrad Ibn ʿUmar calls “Yā Muḥammad” weak

خَدِرَتْ رِجْلُ ابْنِ عُمَرَ، فَقَالَ لَهُ رَجُلٌ: اذْكُرْ أَحَبَّ النَّاسِ إِلَيْكَ، فَقَالَ: يَا مُحَمَّدُ
Ibn ʿUmar's foot went numb. A man said, “Remember the person you love most.” He said, “O Muḥammad.” no. 964

The scholars

From every generation

Companions and Tābiʿūn1st–2nd century

The Companion ʿUthmān b. Ḥunayf narrated the blind man's hadith above. The Companion Bilāl b. al-Ḥārith al-Muzanī is named by Ibn Ḥajar as the man who went to the Prophet's grave in ʿUmar's time (Fatḥ al-Bārī 2/496). Mālik al-Dār, ʿUmar's freed servant, relayed that report; al-Khalīlī calls him a senior tābiʿī “agreed upon” (al-Irshād 1/313).

Imām Mālik2nd century · Mālikī · chain disputed
وَلِمَ تَصْرِفُ وَجْهَكَ عَنْهُ وَهُوَ وَسِيلَتُكَ وَوَسِيلَةُ أَبِيكَ آدَمَ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَامُ إِلَى اللَّهِ تَعَالَى يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ؟ بَلِ اسْتَقْبِلْهُ وَاسْتَشْفِعْ بِهِ فَيُشَفِّعَهُ اللَّهُ

وقد أتيتُك مستغفرًا من ذنوبي مستشفعًا بك إلى ربي

4
Replies to objections

Ibn Taymiyya’s three objections, and the answers

Ibn Taymiyya’s objections are from Majmūʿ al-Fatāwā 1/274–276. The answers are those of the author of the Urdu tafsīr Tibyān al-Qurʾān (vol. 1, pp. 197–198). He first notes that Ibn Taymiyya himself says al-Ṭabarānī’s version has two ṣaḥīḥ chains.

1. “The last words are ʿUthmān’s, not the Prophet’s”“If you have another need, do the same”

Answer: Even if they are ʿUthmān b. Ḥunayf’s words, there is no harm. He was a Companion, and relying on a Companion’s understanding of the Prophet’s teaching is closer to sound reasoning than relying on Ibn Taymiyya’s.

2. “Other narrators don’t have those words”They appear in Ibn Abī Khaythamah’s version

Answer: We do not rely on that version. We rely on al-Ṭabarānī’s report, which Ibn Taymiyya himself says has two ṣaḥīḥ chains.

3. “This only works while the Prophet is praying for the person”and we cannot know he prays for us

Answer: When we ask the Prophet ﷺ to pray for us, Allah makes him aware of it, and he asks Allah to accept it. Nothing in the Sharīʿah or in reason rules this out. Muslim reports that his Ummah’s deeds are shown to him (Abū Dharr, no. 553). The Prophet’s instruction to the blind man has no limit to his lifetime.

5
The most common objection

ʿUmar asked al-ʿAbbās to pray for rain. Doesn’t that close the door?

The reportAnas · al-Bukhārī
أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ ﵁ كَانَ إِذَا قَحَطُوا اسْتَسْقَى بِالْعَبَّاسِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الْمُطَّلِبِ فَقَالَ: اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا كُنَّا نَتَوَسَّلُ إِلَيْكَ بِنَبِيِّنَا فَتَسْقِينَا، وَإِنَّا نَتَوَسَّلُ إِلَيْكَ بِعَمِّ نَبِيِّنَا فَاسْقِنَا، قَالَ فَيُسْقَوْنَ
In years of drought ʿUmar used to ask al-ʿAbbās, the Prophet’s uncle, to pray for rain. He said: “O Allah, we used to turn to You through our Prophet and You would give us rain. Now we turn to You through the uncle of our Prophet, so give us rain.” And they were given rain. Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī no. 1010
The objectionIbn Taymiyya
وَلَوْ كَانَ التَّوَسُّلُ بِهِ بَعْدَ مَوْتِهِ مُمْكِنًا كَالتَّوَسُّلِ بِهِ فِي حَيَاتِهِ لَمَا عَدَلُوا عَنِ الرَّسُولِ ﷺ إِلَى الْعَبَّاسِ
“If turning to Allah through him after his death were possible as it was in his life, they would not have turned from the Messenger ﷺ to al-ʿAbbās.” He reads “through our Prophet” as “through his du’a and intercession”. al-Ikhnāʾiyya p. 463
What the report says, and what it does notReading ʿUmar’s words
Before: in a drought the people went to the Prophet ﷺ himself, and he prayed for them directly. They asked him, “Pray to Allah for us,” and Allah sent rain (Anas, Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim). After he passed away: ʿUmar asked al-ʿAbbās to stand and pray for them directly (ʿAbd al-Razzāq, in Fatḥ al-Bārī 2/495). That is all the report says.
It does not say “you can no longer go to the Prophet and ask for rain”, and no Companion says so. Asking one person to pray does not shut the door on asking another. In the same famine, a Companion went to the Prophet’s grave and said, “O Messenger of Allah, ask Allah for rain for your Ummah.” When ʿUmar was told, he wept and said, “O Lord, I spare no effort except in what I am unable to do.” The report records no objection (Ibn Abī Shaybah; Ibn Ḥajar: “ṣaḥīḥ chain”).

Answers from the scholars who allow it:

1. The words do not say they asked himIbn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī
وَلَيْسَ فِي قَوْلِ عُمَرَ أَنَّهُمْ كَانُوا يَتَوَسَّلُونَ بِهِ دَلَالَةٌ عَلَى أَنَّهُمْ سَأَلُوهُ أَنْ يَسْتَسْقِيَ لَهُمْ، إِذْ يُحْتَمَلُ أَنْ يَكُونُوا فِي الْحَالَيْنِ طَلَبُوا السُّقْيَا مِنَ اللَّهِ مُسْتَشْفِعِينَ بِهِ ﷺ
“There is nothing in ʿUmar’s words ‘we used to turn to You through him’ to show that they asked him to pray for rain. It is possible that, in both situations, they asked Allah for rain, seeking the Prophet’s intercession.” Fatḥ al-Bārī 2/495
2. In the same year, a Companion went to the graveIbn Ḥajar al-ʿAsqalānī
رَوَى ابْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ بِإِسْنَادٍ صَحِيحٍ … فَجَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى قَبْرِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ فَقَالَ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، اسْتَسْقِ لِأُمَّتِكَ فَإِنَّهُمْ قَدْ هَلَكُوا
Ibn Ḥajar places the report of the man at the Prophet’s grave right inside his explanation of this very story. He says it has a ṣaḥīḥ chain, and that the man was the Companion Bilāl b. al-Ḥārith al-Muzanī. The famine, the year and the caliph are the same. 2/495 · 2/496
3. Why al-ʿAbbās, then?Ibn Ḥajar, from al-Zubayr b. Bakkār two chains, narrators disputed
إِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ كَانَ يَرَى لِلْعَبَّاسِ مَا يَرَى الْوَلَدُ لِلْوَالِدِ، فَاقْتَدُوا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ بِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ ﷺ فِي عَمِّهِ الْعَبَّاسِ وَاتَّخِذُوهُ وَسِيلَةً إِلَى اللَّهِ
ʿUmar told the people: “The Messenger of Allah ﷺ treated al-ʿAbbās as a son treats a father. 4. It shows that others may be asked tooAḥmad Daḥlān, as quoted by his critic
